## 🐛 Bugfixes
- Fixed a relatively major bug where the damage vignette (red border) didn't appear after taking damage in most cases.
- Fixed a major bug which lead to players being desynced from their server position, making zombies far away deal damage (Roblox's NextGenerationReplication causes this, we believe)
- Fixed a major vehicle bug where vehicles would launch whenever colliding with trees in a certain way.
- (Hopefully) Fixed a bug where players would get pushed away whenever exiting a vehicle in certain cases.
- Fixed a bug where ghillie suits would cause sparking on vehicles.
- Fixed a bug where a passenger would cause impact sounds on repeat for the driver.
- Fixed a bug where while having the engine UI open on a vehicle then trying to open the storage, nothing would happen.
- Fixed a bug where a vehicle was still simulated by the player when they weren't driving it.
- Fixed a bug where vehicles weren't saving and loading properly in some cases.
- Fixed a bug which made interacting with vehicles difficult in some cases.
- Fixed a bug where a weapon and its ammo were desynced, making it use no ammo.
- Fixed a bug where the stash slots became unresponsive after upgrading stash size.
- Fixed a bug where skinning an item in your inventory which is the same type as one you have equipped would visually skin the one you had equipped at the same time.
- Fixed a bug which caused player locations to be saved after falling off/through the map, leading to an unplayable state.
- Fixed a bug which caused players to spawn in random locations when not spawning in fast enough.
- Fixed a bug which caused stuck UI in the vicinity UI in several cases.
- Fixed a bug where craft max did not craft max in the Crafting UI.
- Fixed a bug where you could not drag/drop an item into the quick craft slots.
- Fixed a bug where you had to press the sprint key twice to sprint.
